A lot of small business owners tell me the same thing: "I know I should be using AI, but I don't have time to figure it out alone." Watching videos at 11 p.m. is not the same as having someone sit next to you and set it up for your actual business.

This week, OpenAI started testing exactly that: free, in-person AI help for small businesses in a handful of US communities. Here's what happened between November 15 and November 27, 2026, what it means for you, and what you can skip.

## Why this week matters

Most AI news is about bigger models and higher prices. This week had something different: a free program aimed at independent small businesses, plus two changes that affect what you already pay for (or might buy). And if you sell toys in New York, there's a new law you need to know about.

## The 5 stories that matter this week

### 1. OpenAI launched OpenWorks: free AI coaching for small businesses (pilot)

On November 23, OpenAI launched **OpenWorks**, a pilot that pairs small businesses with local "OpenWorks Partners" who give free, hands-on AI help ([ZDNET via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/small-business/articles/openai-offering-small-businesses-free-190424906.html)). ZDNET reports it's available in **20 US communities** for now. Forbes names some of them, including Chicago, Houston, New York, Fargo, Incline Village and Charleston ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/11/24/openai-will-pay-ai-experts-up-to-8000-a-month-to-help-small-businesses/)). OpenAI's [OpenWorks page](https://openai.com/openworks/) has a pilot map with the full list.

According to OpenAI's help page ([OpenAI Help](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001724-how-to-apply-to-openworks-and-follow-up-on-your-application)):

- **For businesses:** the pilot is free for independently owned US small businesses and includes **three months of ChatGPT Business**. Nonprofits and faith-based organizations can apply.
- **Not eligible:** software and SaaS companies, venture-backed businesses, and businesses owned by private equity.
- **For people who teach AI:** you can apply to become a partner. Partners are contractors, commit at least **four full days a month plus one monthly workshop**, work in person in the US, and get a stipend of **up to $8,000 a month**. OpenAI doesn't say how the amount is calculated.
- **No guarantee:** OpenAI says it "can't promise that OpenWorks will launch in your area."

**Cost:** 🆓 Free during the pilot. After the 3 free months, ChatGPT Business costs 💳 $25 per user per month billed monthly, or $20 billed annually, with a 2-user minimum ([OpenAI pricing](https://openai.com/business/pricing/)). Free alternative: the free ChatGPT plan.

### 2. ChatGPT usage limits are becoming one shared "meter" in December

On November 25, TechCrunch reported that OpenAI is merging its Chat and Work experiences, starting with a small group of Pro subscribers ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/25/openai-says-heavy-chatgpt-users-may-hit-limits-sooner-as-it-merges-chat-and-work/)). An OpenAI spokesperson said Chat and Work "will share a single usage meter," while conversations with the **Instant** model stay unlimited. OpenAI's business pricing page confirms that starting in December, plan usage will have a shared limit across ChatGPT and Codex (OpenAI's coding tool).

What it means in plain words: if you use ChatGPT heavily for chat **and** for longer tasks, both now come out of the same allowance. OpenAI says most users won't notice, but its heaviest users may hit limits sooner.

**Cost:** No price change was announced.

### 3. Claude can turn your artifacts into desktop apps (beta)

An "artifact" is something Claude builds for you, like a small calculator, tracker or tool. SiliconANGLE reported on November 24 that Anthropic added **Install as App**, which turns an artifact into its own desktop app on Windows and Mac, with its own icon and window ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/11/24/anthropic-is-trying-to-kill-traditional-desktop-apps-with-claude-generated-desktop-apps/)).

- It's a beta for Claude subscribers. On Team plans it's on by default; Enterprise admins must turn it on.
- You must be **online and signed in** to open the app.
- Requests the app makes to Claude **count toward your usage**.

**Cost:** 💳 Paid. Claude Pro is $20/month ($17/month billed annually) ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). Free alternative: keep using your artifact in the browser, or a simple Google Sheet.

### 4. Gemini can now read your Google Wallet (if you turn it on)

Google now lets you connect **Google Wallet** to the Gemini app so you can ask questions about spending and subscriptions ([Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/google-wallet-gemini-app/)). Ghacks reports it's rolling out to Gemini app users in the US, it's **off by default**, and Gemini gets **read-only** access: it can't move money or complete transactions ([Ghacks](https://www.ghacks.net/2026/11/15/gemini-can-now-read-your-google-wallet-to-track-spending-and-subscriptions-but-it-cannot-move-money/)).

My take: it can help you spot forgotten subscriptions. But if you mix business and personal cards, think twice before connecting financial data to any AI tool. You can switch it off later in the same settings menu.

**Cost:** 🆓 Free in the Gemini app (US).

### 5. New York banned AI chatbot toys for five years

If you run a gift shop, toy store or online store that ships to New York, this one is for you. The state's chatbot toy bill (S9408A) was **signed on November 24, 2026** ([NY Senate](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S9408)). It bans manufacturing, distributing, holding for sale or selling toys with an embedded AI companion for children. The ban starts **90 days after it became law** and expires after five years. The Attorney General can seek penalties of up to **$15,000 per day for each violation**.

This is not legal advice. If you stock "smart" or talking toys, check your inventory and ask a lawyer how the final text applies to you.

## The opportunity: apply for OpenWorks (or nominate someone)

This is the week's one free, practical move. Here's how, based on OpenAI's help page:

1. **Open the [OpenWorks page](https://openai.com/openworks/)** and look at the pilot map.
2. **Pick your form:** "Get help for your business," "Apply to become a partner," or "Nominate a business or partner."
3. **Submit it.** The message on screen is your confirmation. If they move forward, they'll email you.
4. **Don't wait by the inbox.** OpenAI hasn't published a way to update your application yet, so fill it in carefully the first time.

### Before you apply, write down

- [ ] What your business does and who you serve, in one sentence
- [ ] The 3 tasks that eat most of your week (emails, quotes, scheduling, social posts)
- [ ] What tools you already use (Gmail, Square, QuickBooks, Google Sheets)
- [ ] One thing you'd love AI to take off your plate first

### Copy-paste prompt to get ready (works in any free AI tool)

```
I run a small business and I'm applying for free AI coaching.
Help me explain my needs clearly.

My business: [what you do, where, for whom]
Tasks that take most of my time: [list 3]
Tools I use now: [list]
My comfort with AI: [none / a little / I use it weekly]

Write:
1. A 3-sentence description of my business and my biggest time drains
2. The 3 AI projects that would help me most, in plain language
3. 5 questions I should ask an AI coach in our first session
Don't invent facts about my business.
```

If you teach AI or tech to others, the partner track is worth a look. Read the time commitment carefully, though: four full days a month is a lot if you already run a business.

## The honest truth

- **OpenWorks is a pilot.** It's only in some communities, OpenAI hasn't said how long it will last, and applying doesn't mean you'll be accepted.
- **Free months end.** Put a reminder on your calendar before month four so you decide on purpose whether to keep paying.
- **"Up to $8,000" is not a salary promise.** OpenAI hasn't explained how partner pay is set.
- **Don't connect money data just because you can.** Read-only is safer than full access, but it's still your financial information.
- **The basics still matter most.** A coach can help, but clear processes and a complete Google Business Profile do more than any new feature.

### 3. Claude puede convertir tus artefactos en aplicaciones de escritorio (beta)

Un "artefacto" es algo que Claude construye para ti, como una calculadora, un registro o una herramienta pequeña. - Es una beta para suscriptores de Claude. En los planes Team viene activada; en Enterprise, el administrador tiene que activarla.

- Lo que la app le pide a Claude **cuenta para tu uso**.

**Costo:** 💳 De pago. Claude Pro cuesta $20 al mes ($17 al mes con pago anual) ([precios de Claude](https://claude.com/pricing)). Alternativa gratis: sigue usando tu artefacto en el navegador, o una hoja sencilla de Google Sheets.

### 5. Nueva York prohibió los juguetes con chatbot de IA por cinco años

Si tienes una tienda de regalos, una juguetería o una tienda en línea que envía a Nueva York, esto te interesa. El proyecto de ley sobre juguetes con chatbot (S9408A) se **firmó el 24 de noviembre de 2026** ([Senado de NY](https://www.nysenate.gov/legislation/bills/2025/S9408)). Prohíbe fabricar, distribuir, tener a la venta o vender juguetes para niños con un compañero de IA integrado. La prohibición empieza **90 días después de convertirse en ley** y vence a los cinco años. La Fiscalía General puede pedir multas de hasta **$15,000 por día por cada infracción**.

Esto no es asesoría legal. Si vendes juguetes "inteligentes" o que hablan, revisa tu inventario y pregúntale a un abogado cómo te aplica el texto final.

## La verdad, sin rodeos

- **OpenWorks es un piloto.** Solo está en algunas comunidades, OpenAI no ha dicho cuánto va a durar y aplicar no significa que te acepten.
- **Los meses gratis se acaban.** Pon un recordatorio en tu calendario antes del cuarto mes para decidir a conciencia si sigues pagando.
- **"Hasta $8,000" no es una promesa de sueldo.** OpenAI no ha explicado cómo se fija el pago de los partners.
- **No conectes tus datos de dinero solo porque se puede.** Solo lectura es más seguro que acceso total, pero sigue siendo tu información financiera.
- **Lo básico sigue pesando más.** Un asesor ayuda, pero tener procesos claros y un Perfil de Negocio de Google completo hace más que cualquier función nueva.
